# Cards the game doesn't automate yet

Every Trainer, Ability and Special Energy in the whole card pool (all 20,635 cards, every format) that still falls through to the "isn't automated" message, checked on 2026-10-06 against the rules engine in `src/shared/practice`. Reprints with the same name are listed once. When one of these is played, the By hand moves open so the player can do what the card says.

**Totals:** 993 Trainer cards, 1625 Abilities (including the older Poké-Powers, Poké-Bodies and Pokémon Powers), 84 Special Energy.

**Standard-legal right now:** 11 Trainers (Ange Floette, Antique Armor Fossil, Antique Skull Fossil, Gladion's Final Battle, Great Haul Net, Quad Stone, Roseanne's Backup, Rust Syndicate Grunt, Serena, Special Red Card, Transformation Tome) and 5 Special Energy (Bubbly Water Energy, Magnetic Metal Energy, Nitro Fire Energy, Shadowy Darkness Energy, Voltaic Lightning Energy). No Standard Abilities are missing. These all come from the newest sets, released after the last automation pass.

**Expanded-legal:** 618 Trainers, 790 Abilities, 55 Special Energy.

Special Energy that isn't listed here is automated. An unautomated Special Energy still provides 1 Colorless Energy, but the rest of its text is done by hand.
